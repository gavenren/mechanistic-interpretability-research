# Primary sources and overlap

Checked 13 October 2026. This is a focused search, not a systematic review. No exact match to the full access-by-coordinate factorial was found in the checked material. That is not proof of novelty. The study does not claim to invent rotary position edits, retrieval heads, or a separation between phase and contextual content.

1. **Tom Zehle and Matthias Aßenmacher (2026). [Can Calibration of Positional Encodings Enhance Long Context Utilization?](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-eacl.120/)** Findings of EACL, pages 2268–2280. The primary PDF and method discussion were checked. CALIOPE remaps rotary positions during inference and tests retrieval and question answering with distractors. It also discusses softmax flattening. This is close prior work for the coordinate intervention. Our test adds a crossed, all-layer access mask and two short-input equivalence controls. It is not a new position calibrator.

2. **Julie Huang, Maggie Chlon, Gregory Gutin, and Leon Chlon (2026). [Exact Finite Attention Responses From RoPE Derivatives](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14127v1).** Version 1, 12 September 2026. The primary full-text experimental discussion was checked. It studies finite phase edits, key/value edits, and downstream response predictions. It compares frozen-content phase edits with real prompt reordering, where contextual features also change. Thus this distinction is already known. Our coordinate effect includes those later changes. The all-layer access-mask factorial is a different, narrower diagnostic.

3. **Eric Enouen and Sainyam Galhotra (2026). [Shifting Mechanisms: How Positional Encoding Choice Shapes In-Context Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38530v1).** Version 1, 29 September 2026. Mechanism and architecture sections were checked. The work compares positional, lexical, and reflexive retrieval mechanisms, including competing keys and encoding-dependent behavior. Our test is one controlled comparison in one checkpoint. It does not establish a new retrieval taxonomy or a general ranking of positional encodings.

4. **Cengizhan Bayram (2026). [Does RoPE Prevent or Degrade Retrieval Heads? A Mechanistic Analysis Across Model Families](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21249).** Primary abstract checked. It reports head masking and rotary-frequency interventions across models. Causal tests of rotary coordinates and retrieval components are therefore prior work. Abstract-only checking limits the detail of our comparison.

5. **Stella Biderman and colleagues (2023). [Pythia: A Suite for Analyzing Large Language Models Across Training and Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01373v2).** Version 2, 31 May 2023; primary abstract and author metadata checked. Pythia supplies related public models and training checkpoints for model analysis. The 14M model was added after the original suite. Model-specific claims here use the pinned card, not an assumption that every detail of the original paper applies to 14M.

6. **EleutherAI. [Pythia-14M model card at the pinned revision](https://huggingface.co/EleutherAI/pythia-14m/blob/cf967c0a9a04383db6f7b1108d86b2962634b4ac/README.md).** The card specifies Apache 2.0. It says that the URL changed on 27 February 2026: the earlier model trained on the deduplicated Pile moved to a separate repository. This study uses the immutable revision and file hashes in the runner. It uses the standard-Pile model at that revision. The card was read locally from the hash-checked download. Do not describe the checkpoint by the mutable repository name alone.

7. **Hugging Face Transformers, [GPT-NeoX implementation, version 4.57.6](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/v4.57.6/src/transformers/models/gpt_neox/modeling_gpt_neox.py).** The installed matching source was checked. The model adds parallel attention and MLP outputs to the residual. Stock eager attention forces a float32 softmax; this experiment changes that softmax to the query dtype. Stock rotary positions and trigonometric tables still use float32. The causal mask uses physical sequence order in our runner, separate from the supplied rotary coordinates.

8. **This repository, [copy-routing study](../2026-10-13-copy-routing/paper.md).** This is the source of the preselected zero-based block 3, head 1. It is a prior AI-authored experiment in the same model, not independent literature. The new token pool does not reuse its 192 eligible token IDs. The new experiment does not select a new head on its test data.

The bounded contribution is the fixed 2×2 intervention grid, its exact deletion controls, and the measured result on a new set of token groups. A negative result is publishable if the controls pass. Neither a positive nor a negative result can explain long-context behavior across models.
