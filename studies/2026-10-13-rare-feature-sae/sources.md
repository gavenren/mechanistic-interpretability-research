# Sources and the scope of the contribution

Primary sources checked on 13 October 2026. This is a targeted search. It is not a systematic review and does not prove that a result is the first of its kind.

| Source | What it establishes | Relationship to this study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dan Braun, Jordan Taylor, Nicholas Goldowsky-Dill, and Lee Sharkey. [Identifying Functionally Important Features with End-to-End Sparse Dictionary Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12241), 2024, arXiv v2 | SAE training can include the model's downstream output loss. | Our ordinary downstream objective follows this established approach. Label balancing is an oracle comparison in a small controlled setting. |
| Leo Gao et al. [Scaling and Evaluating Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04093), 2024 | TopK provides direct control of a sparse code budget; feature quality needs several forms of evaluation. | We use TopK and keep its maximum code size fixed. We do not claim a new SAE architecture. |
| David Chanin and Adrià Garriga-Alonso. [SynthSAEBench: Evaluating Sparse Autoencoders on Scalable Realistic Synthetic Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14687), 2026 | Synthetic ground truth can distinguish reconstruction quality from feature recovery. | Our generator isolates one specific comparison. It is much smaller and intentionally simpler than a general SAE benchmark. |
| David Chanin and Adrià Garriga-Alonso. [Sparse but Wrong: Incorrect L0 Leads to Incorrect Features in Sparse Autoencoders](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/chanin26a.html), ICML 2026 | Choosing a sparsity level can affect which features are learned. | We report the fixed budget as a limit and also measure actual code activity. |
| Mohamed Abdessalem Bal. [From Geometric Recovery to Causal Validation: A Reproducible Audit of Sparse Autoencoder Features, from Superposition Geometry to Causal Inertness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12166), July 2026 | A decoder direction can align with a known feature while the associated latent fails to have the expected causal use. | We test a learned latent by intervention. Cosine and activation statistics are secondary. |
| Phu Gia Hoang, Anwoy Chatterjee, Tanmoy Chakraborty, Iryna Gurevych, and Subhabrata Dutta. [Sparse Autoencoders Encode Both Concepts and Functions: The Downstream Geometry of Feature Effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24645), July 2026 | A feature's effects can depend on context, even when its activation has a clear interpretation. | Our paired-background and same-label controls test a narrow form of context dependence. They do not reproduce the paper's general geometry analysis. |

The defensible contribution is the locked, paired experiment: vary feature frequency and amplitude independently; hold the true output change constant; compare three learned SAE objectives; and test a validation-selected latent on separate counterfactual pairs. The broad concerns and main tools were already known. This study does not establish their frequency in language models.
