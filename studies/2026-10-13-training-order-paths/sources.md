# Source record

Search date: 13 October 2026. These are primary sources. The abstracts and publication records were checked before the study. The search was focused, not exhaustive. No claim of first discovery is supported by this record.

| Source | Checked version | Relevance and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wang and Murfet, [Patterning: The Dual of Interpretability](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wang26fw.html) | ICML 2026 proceedings | Data weighting changes circuit formation and selects algorithms. Our narrow change is fixed example counts with different order and a common tail. Training data as a causal control is established. |
| Chen, Luo, and Pan, [Mechanistic Data Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21996v2) | v2, 7 June 2026 | Studies training origins of interpretable units and changes influential data. We do not introduce a new data-to-mechanism link. |
| Odonnat, Bouaziz, and Cabannes, [Easing Optimization Paths: a Circuit Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02362v1) | v1, 4 January 2025 | Connects curricula and circuit learning in controlled settings. Training order and mechanisms are not new topics. |
| Achille, Rovere, and Soatto, [Critical Learning Periods in Deep Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08856) | arXiv abstract record | Early training conditions can have lasting effects. A finite balanced tail cannot establish a new critical period or irreversible loss. |
| Lyle et al., [What can grokking teach us about learning under non-stationarity?](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v330/lyle26a.html) | PMLR 330, 2026 | Studies feature learning and overwriting under changing training conditions. This is close motivation. |
| Sevetlidis and Pavlidis, [Process-Tensor Tomography of SGD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16563v1) | v1, 23 January 2026 | Tests order memory and optimizer-state resets. Our reset control and training-history question are not new principles. We make no non-Markovianity claim. |
| Nikishin et al., [The Primacy Bias in Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/nikishin22a.html) | ICML 2022 proceedings | Studies early-data effects and resets, including optimizer statistics. We use a small supervised parity task with exact input enumeration. |
| Asadi, Fakoor, and Sabach, [Resetting the Optimizer in Deep RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17833) | arXiv abstract record | Optimizer reset is an established intervention. It does not erase the training history already stored in weights. |

The intended contribution is one fully specified matched-example benchmark with a fixed internal path measure, complete counterfactual inputs, and a crossed reset control. It does not claim a new localization algorithm, a general training-order effect, or a result about large language models.
