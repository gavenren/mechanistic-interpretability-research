The numerical argument is elementary. For a q-contraction, the triangle inequality gives `||h-z*|| <= ||h-F(h)|| + q||h-z*||`. Rearranging gives the standard residual error bound. Applying the dual-norm inequality to a fixed linear readout and adding the intact and edited errors gives the effect bound. These are not claimed as new theorems. Outward rounding is needed to turn the real-arithmetic expressions into computed enclosures.

We found no exact implementation of this complete internal-effect sign audit in these methods. That is a limited search conclusion, not a priority guarantee. The useful output would be checked interval code, complete trained-model effect records, and an honest count of certified, small, unresolved, and contradicted signs.
